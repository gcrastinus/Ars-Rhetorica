How to use

Select one or more exercises from the course, choose the difficulty and how many questions you want from each, then generate. Most items are drawn half from the passages the students drill in the app, and half from a hidden corpus used only here, so a printed quiz is not a reprint of last week’s set. Where the work itself is small (Gorgias’s Helen is six passages; Antiphon’s tetralogy is ten), the quiz must use that work — there is no honest second Helen.

You may add doctrine questions (definition, the three means of persuasion, the offices of the oration, and the like) from the divisions you have selected. Two is the minimum if you include them. The answer key prints on its own page(s) — separate those pages before handing the quiz out. Use the browser’s Print → Save as PDF.
